The Challenge

Wound Care Today Is Reactive. Patients Pay the Price.

Every day, clinicians make critical wound care decisions using information that reflects the past rather than predicts the future.

Assessments are performed.

Photos are documented.

Treatments are delivered.

Yet wound deterioration can begin days before visible warning signs appear.

By the time changes become obvious:

  • ✕
    Clinical intervention becomes more difficult
  • ✕
    Costs significantly increase
  • ✕
    Preventable complications emerge
  • ✕
    Documentation gaps create survey risk
  • ✕
    Patients experience avoidable setbacks
The issue is not clinician expertise.
Traditional systems only show what has already happened.

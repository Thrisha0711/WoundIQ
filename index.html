Platform Capabilities

Built for Real Clinical Environments.

Wound-IQ is not a concept or a prototype. Every capability below is fully built, deployed, and operational — designed specifically for Skilled Nursing Facilities, Assisted Living Facilities, Wound Care Clinics, and Home Healthcare Providers.

01

AI-Assisted Wound Analysis

Capture wound images on any mobile device. AI analyzes dimensions, tissue type, healing progression, and risk indicators — supporting clinicians with structured, data-driven insight.

02

PUSH Score Trending

Automatic PUSH Tool 3.0 scoring on every assessment (0–17 scale). Color-coded alerts flag wounds that are stalling or deteriorating — so your team can act before it escalates.

03

Braden Scale Risk Assessment

Server-side rule engine evaluates 6 subscales for pressure injury risk. Clinician-guided scoring with override capability. Automatically triggers MNA nutritional screening when risk score ≤ 11.

04

11-Area Structured Assessment

One comprehensive form captures measurements, tissue analysis, exudate, wound edges, periwound skin, tunneling, pain, infection status, and treatment plan — replacing inconsistent free-text charting.

05

MNA Nutritional Screening

Two-phase Mini Nutritional Assessment with server-computed BMI. Automatically gates to full MNA when screening indicates risk. Supports MDS 3.0 Section K nutritional documentation requirements.

06

FHIR R4 Interoperability

EHR-ready data export with LOINC, SNOMED CT, and ICD-10-CM coding. US Core profiles for Patient, Condition, and Observation. Designed for seamless PCC and EHR system integration.

07

Multi-Role Clinical Workflow

Role-based access for Admin, Clinician, Nurse, Scheduler, and Patient — with row-level data scoping. Structured review workflow: Draft → Submitted → Under Review → Finalized → Locked.

08

HIPAA-Aligned Security

All data encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Multi-tenant isolation with tenant ID enforced on every query. JWT authentication with Argon2id password hashing. Hosted on Microsoft Azure.
